Privacy Policy

GWLabs Codera · Last updated 20 September 2026

Codera is a place to watch, post and learn from coding tutorials — on the web, on Android, on iPhone, iPad and Vision Pro, and on the desktop. It is made by GWLabs ("we"). This policy says what we collect, why, who else sees it, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your email address, the username you pick, and — if you sign in with Google or GitHub — the name and picture those services share with us. We never see your Google or GitHub password.
  • What you post. Posts, code, pictures, videos, shorts, comments, likes and dislikes, who you follow, your profile picture, banner and description, live stream titles and live chat messages.
  • What you watch. The topics of what you watch, like, search for and post (for example "Python"), kept with your account so Home can show you more of what you want to learn. This stays in your account and is not shown to anyone else.
  • Payments. If you subscribe to Codera Plus or tip a streamer, the payment is handled by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us; we keep only what we need to know the payment happened (such as Stripe customer and payment identifiers and whether your subscription is active). Streamers who set up payouts give their identity and bank details to Stripe directly; we learn only whether payouts are ready.
  • Technical information. Like any website or app, our servers receive your IP address and basic device and browser details when you use Codera. Live video travels directly between the streamer's and the viewer's devices, so each can see the other's IP address.

We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.

What is public

Your username, profile picture, banner, description, everything you post, your comments, live chat messages and how many people follow you can be seen by other people using Codera. Your email address is never shown.

How we use it

  • To run your account and show what you post to others.
  • To order your Home feed by what you're interested in.
  • To process Codera Plus subscriptions and tips, and to pay streamers.
  • To keep Codera safe: stopping spam and abuse and enforcing our Terms.

Ads

Codera shows a small number of ads — in the Android app through Google AdMob, and on the website through Google's IMA SDK. Google may use cookies or your device's advertising identifier to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time. You can also limit ad tracking in your device's settings. Codera Plus members see no ads.

Who else handles your data

We do not sell your personal information. We use these services to run Codera, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase — accounts, database, file storage and hosting.
  • Stripe — payments, subscriptions and streamer payouts.
  • Google AdMob and Google Ad Manager — ads, for people without Codera Plus.
  • Google and GitHub — only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Yoti — only if you're asked to confirm your age (see below).

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect people from harm.

Keeping and deleting

You can delete your own posts at any time. To delete your whole account and everything you've posted, email us at the address below and we will do it within 30 days. Payment records may be kept longer where the law requires it (for example for tax), and by Stripe under its own policy.

Children and age checks

Codera is not for children under 13. If your country sets a higher age for using services like this without a parent's permission, you must be at least that age. If you believe a child under that age has an account, contact us.

When you make an account we ask your date of birth. We do not store that date: we work out the day you turn 18 and keep only that. Nobody but you can read it, and it is never shown on your profile. On its own it does not unlock anything — a date anyone can type is not proof of age. Links a creator puts on their profile lead off Codera, so both putting them up and opening them require a Yoti face check, and the result of that check is the only thing that opens them.

If we have reason to think an account belongs to someone under the minimum age — for example from reports, or from what's said, shown or heard in their posts, videos or streams — we may ask them to confirm their age before they can keep using Codera. That check is done by Yoti, an independent age-verification service, in one of two ways: a face scan that estimates age, or a photo of an ID document. Yoti handles the image and tells us only whether the person meets the age limit; Codera never receives or keeps the face scan or the ID, and Yoti deletes them once the check is done, under its own privacy policy. If the check isn't completed, or shows the person is under the minimum age, the account may be limited or removed.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or object to how it is used. Email us and we will help. You can also change your profile, and stop using Google or GitHub sign-in, at any time.

Security

Codera runs on Google's infrastructure with encrypted connections, and our database rules only let each person change their own things. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep it to yourself.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the app and on this page before the change takes effect.
