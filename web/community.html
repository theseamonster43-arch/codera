Community Standards

GWLabs Codera · Last updated 20 September 2026

Codera is for learning to code. Everything posted here — posts, shorts, videos, live streams, comments and chat — should help someone learn, build or understand something about programming and technology. These standards say what belongs on Codera, what doesn't, and what happens when something breaks them. They're part of our Terms of Service.

1. Stay on topic

Codera is about coding and technology. That includes:

  • tutorials, walkthroughs and explanations of code in any language;
  • questions and answers about programming, tools, errors and setups;
  • building projects, games, apps, websites, hardware and robots — and showing how;
  • computer science, maths for programming, security, AI and data;
  • developer tools, editors, devices and careers in tech;
  • live coding streams, and saved streams of them.

Not on topic: vlogs, pranks, gaming footage with no coding in it, music, memes, reaction videos, "test" or empty posts, and anything else that isn't about making or understanding technology. Gameplay is fine when it's part of building or modding the game.

2. Keep it appropriate

People of all ages learn here, many of them students. Don't post, stream or send:

  • nudity, sexual content or sexual comments — and never anything sexual involving minors;
  • graphic violence, gore, self-harm, or anything encouraging it;
  • hate, harassment, bullying or threats against anyone;
  • drugs, weapons or other dangerous activities presented as something to try;
  • swearing aimed at people, or content made to shock;
  • scams, spam, misleading links or impersonation.

3. Code that's safe to learn from

  • Don't share malware, credential stealers or code meant to harm the people who run it.
  • Security topics are welcome when they're clearly educational — explain what the code does and never target real people or systems you don't own.
  • Never post passwords, API keys or other secrets — yours or anyone's.

4. Respect people and their work

  • Only post code, video and music you made or have the right to share, and credit what you build on.
  • Don't share someone's personal information.
  • Disagree with ideas, not with people. Be kind to beginners — everyone started somewhere.

5. Live streams and chat

Everything above applies live. Streamers are responsible for what's on their stream and can delete messages in their chat. Tips are for supporting creators — don't ask for tips in exchange for anything that breaks these standards.

How we enforce them

  • Checked when posted. New posts and videos are screened automatically, and anything that looks off-topic or inappropriate is held back from feeds until it's reviewed.
  • Reports. Anyone can report a post, video, stream or message. Things reported by several people are hidden while we look at them.
  • Age checks. Codera is for people 13 and over (or older where the law says so). If an account looks like it belongs to someone younger — from reports, or what's said, shown or heard on it — we may ask the owner to confirm their age with a quick face-based age estimate or an ID check through Yoti. Codera never sees the scan or the ID; we only learn whether the age limit is met.
  • What happens. Content that breaks these standards is removed or kept off feeds, and you're told why. Repeated or serious breaks lead to your account being limited, suspended or removed. Anything illegal, especially involving the safety of children, is reported to the authorities.
  • If we got it wrong. You can ask for a review of anything held back or removed, and a person will look at it.
